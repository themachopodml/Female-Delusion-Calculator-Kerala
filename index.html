Data status
Census 2011 remains the latest completed population census. Census 2027 is underway, but final enumeration results are not available yet. The calculator uses Census 2011 classifications plus official population projections where needed. NFHS-6 (2023–24), released in May 2026, reports Kerala men aged 15–49 at 37.0% overweight or obese, so Exclude uses 63.0% as a non-overweight/non-obese screening proxy. PLFS 2025 is the latest annual labour/earnings release. High-income and asset intersections are modelled because no government table publishes the exact combined matrimonial population.

Sources: Census of India / ORGI; Government of India Population Projection 2011–2036; MoSPI PLFS 2025; MoHFW & IIPS NFHS-6 2023–24; Kerala State Planning Board Economic Review 2025.
