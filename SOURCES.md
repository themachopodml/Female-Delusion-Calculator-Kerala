## Modelling policy

Official figures are used where a directly relevant published statistic exists. Where no government table provides the requested intersection or threshold, the calculator uses an explicit modelled screening factor. Modelled factors are not presented as official Census counts.

In particular:
- high annual-income tiers are modelled scarcity thresholds;
- asset ownership intersections are modelled;
- the height envelope is a calculator-defined range;
- the final intersection assumes multiplicative screening and therefore is an estimate.
